Lab 3: Payment Plan Design

Scenario: Three patients are experiencing financial difficulties and cannot pay their outstanding balances in full upon receipt of statement. Structure fair, compliant, and realistic monthly installment plans while applying clinic financial advocacy policies.

Deliverable: Patient Payment Plan Worksheet Skills: Patient Advocacy • Collections Management • Problem Solving

Clinic Payment Policy Parameters

  • Balances under $300: 2 to 5 monthly installments (Minimum $50/month).
  • Balances $300 - $1,000: 4 to 8 monthly installments (Minimum $100/month).
  • Balances over $1,000: 6 to 12 monthly installments (Minimum $125/month).
  • Zero interest or finance fees charged to patient.
  • Requires signed Promissory Agreement with automatic monthly debit authorization.
